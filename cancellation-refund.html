Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Plans change, we understand. This page explains, in simple words, what happens if you cancel a trip booked with Bharat Darshanam Travels.

If you cancel

The refund depends on how many days before the departure date you tell us. "Trip cost" means the full price of your trip.

When you cancelWhat you get back
30 days or more before departureThe amount you paid, minus 10% of the trip cost
15 to 29 days before departure50% of the trip cost (we keep 50% of the trip cost from what you paid)
7 to 14 days before departure25% of the trip cost (we keep 75% of the trip cost from what you paid)
Less than 7 days before departure, or no-showNo refund

Example: Your trip cost is ₹20,000 and you paid it in full. You cancel 40 days before departure. You get back ₹20,000 − ₹2,000 (10%) = ₹18,000.

If what you paid is less than the amount we keep, there is no refund, and nothing more to pay.

Free option: transfer or move your booking

Instead of cancelling, you can do one of these for free:

  • Give your booking to a friend (send us their name, phone and ID before the trip).
  • Move to a later departure of the same trip.

Both depend on seats being available.

Weather, landslides and government orders

Sometimes a trip has to change or end early because of weather, landslides, roadblocks or government orders. This is outside our control. In such cases there is no refund for services you could not use. If a hotel, transport or other vendor refunds us any money, we pass all of it on to you.

If we cancel the trip

If we cancel a departure for our own reasons (for example, too few travellers), you can choose a full refund of the amount you paid or move to another date.

How to cancel

Send your cancellation on WhatsApp to +91 95113 33174 or by email to info@bharatdarshanamtravels.com, with your name, trip and departure date. The date we receive your message is the date we use to count the days.

When you get the money

Refunds go back to the same account or card you paid from, within 7 working days after we confirm the cancellation.
