Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Bharat Darshanam Travels (bharatdarshanamtravels.com), run by Gajendra Singh, respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your name, phone number, email, the trip you are interested in, travel dates, number of travellers and any message you send, through our forms, WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • For bookings: details needed for the trip, such as ID details of travellers when hotels or permits need them.
  • Payments: online payments are handled by our payment partner. We get a confirmation of payment, but we never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and plan your trip.
  • To confirm bookings, take payments and send trip updates.
  • To book hotels, transport and permits for you.
  • To send you trip offers, only if you agree. You can say stop any time.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with hotels, transport providers, local guides and permit offices for your trip, and with our payment partner and email service to run the website. We never sell your information.

Services we use

  • Our enquiry form sends your message to our email through Web3Forms.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

How long we keep it

We keep your details as long as needed for your trip and for our accounts and legal records. You can ask us to delete your details any time, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Write to info@bharatdarshanamtravels.com or WhatsApp +91 95113 33174.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
