Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 26 November 2026

These terms apply when you book a trip with Bharat Darshanam Travels (bharatdarshanamtravels.com), run by Gajendra Singh. By booking, you agree to them. Please read them once; they are short.

1. Booking and payment

  • To book, you can pay a 30% advance or the full amount.
  • If you pay the advance, the balance is due before departure, on the date given in your booking confirmation.
  • Your seat is confirmed once we receive the payment and send you a confirmation.
  • Prices are per person in Indian Rupees. We are not GST registered yet, so no GST is added.
  • Online payments are handled by our payment partner. We never see or store your card or bank details.

2. What the price includes

Each trip page lists what is included and what is not. Anything not listed as included (for example, personal expenses, extra meals or entry tickets not mentioned) is not part of the price.

3. Cancellation and refunds

See our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

4. Changes to the trip

We try hard to follow the itinerary. Sometimes we may need to change the plan, hotel or route because of weather, road conditions, safety or local rules. When this happens, we will give a similar option where possible.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Carry a valid government photo ID for the whole trip.
  • Tell us before booking about any health condition that matters for the trip, especially for treks and high-altitude trips.
  • Follow the trip leader's safety instructions, local laws and respect local culture.
  • We may remove a traveller from the trip, without refund, for unsafe behaviour, harassment or illegal activity.

6. Safety and liability

Travel, and especially trekking and mountain travel, carries some risk. We take reasonable care and work with trusted local partners, but we are not responsible for loss, injury, delay or extra cost caused by things outside our control, such as weather, natural events, strikes, government orders or the acts of third parties. We suggest you take travel insurance.

7. Photos

We may take photos during trips and use them on our website and social media. If you do not want to appear, just tell your trip leader.

8. Law

These terms follow Indian law. Any dispute will be handled by the competent courts in India.
